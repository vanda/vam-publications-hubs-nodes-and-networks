Adler, P. et al. (eds.) (2013) Art and the Internet. London: Black Dog Publishing.

Benthall, J. (1972) *Science and Technology in Art Today*. London: Thames & Hudson.

Brown, P. (2008) *White Heat Cold Logic: British Computer Art 1960–1980*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

Connor, M.J., Dean, A. and Espenschied, D. (eds.) (2019) *The Art Happens Here: Net Art Anthology*. New York: Rhizome.

Giannetti, C. (ed.) (2023) *Analivia Cordeiro: From Body to Code. Karlsruhe: ZKM/Center for Art and Media*; Munich: Hirmer Verlag.

Curtis, D. (2020) *London’s Arts Labs, and the 60s Avant-Garde*. Exeter: John Libbey Publishing.

Dekker, A. (ed.) (2021) *Curating Digital Art: From Presenting and Collecting Digital Art to Networked Co-curation*. Amsterdam: Valiz.

Druckrey, T. (ed.) (1999) *Ars Electronica: Facing the Future, a Survey of Two Decades*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

Franco, F. (2022) *The Algorithmic Dimension: Five Artists in Conversation*. Cham: Springer.

Franke, H.W. (1971) *Computer Graphics, Computer Art*. London: Phaidon.

Greene, R. (2004) *Internet Art*. London: Thames & Hudson.

Jones, L. (ed.) (2023) *Coded: Art Enters the Computer Age, 1952–1982*. Los Angeles: DelMonico Books and Los Angeles County Museum of Art.

Kahn, D. and Higgins, H. (eds.) (2012) *Mainframe Experimentalism: Early Computing and the Foundations of the Digital Arts*. Berkeley: University of California Press.

Latham, W. (1989) *The Conquest of Form: Computer Art by William Latham*. Bristol: Arnolfini Gallery

Mason, C. (2008) *A Computer in the Art Room: The Origins of British Computer Arts 1950–80*. Hindringham, Norfolk: JJG.

McCray, W.P. (2020) *Making Art Work: How Cold War Engineers and Artists Forged a New Creative Culture*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

Paul, C. (2003) *Digital Art*. London: Thames & Hudson.

Patterson, Z. (2015) *Peripheral Vision: Bell Labs, the S-C 4020, and the Origins of Computer Art*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

Reichardt, J. (1971) *The Computer in Art*. 1st edn. London: Studio Vista/Van Nostrand Reinhold.

Reichardt, J. (ed.) (1968) *Cybernetic Serendipity: The Computer and the Arts*. Studio International, special issue. New York and London.

Rosen, M. et al. (2011) *A Little-Known Story about a Movement, a Magazine and the Computer’s Arrival in Art: New Tendencies and Bit International, 1961–1973*. Karlsruhe: ZKM/Center for Art and Media; Cambridge, MA: MIT Press.

Taylor, G.D. (2014) *When the Machine Made Art: The Troubled History of Computer Art*. London: Bloomsbury.

Todd, S.J.P. and Latham, W. (1992) *Evolutionary Art and Computers*. London: Academic Press.
