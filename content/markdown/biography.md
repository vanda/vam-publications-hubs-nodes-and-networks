**Pita Arreola** Pita Arreola is a London-based curator specialised in digital and media art. She is currently Head of Programmes at arebyte Digital Art Centre, London. With fifteen years of experience in the arts, she has worked closely with creatives to develop experimental projects that critically examine the social and cultural impact of emerging technologies. She is also the Co-Founder of Off Site Project, a curatorial platform supporting new media talent. From 2021 to 2024, she was Curator of Digital Art at the Victoria and Albert Museum. She is co-editor of *Digital Art: 1960s to Now* (V&A/Thames & Hudson, 2024), a book tracing the histories and evolution of digital arts.
